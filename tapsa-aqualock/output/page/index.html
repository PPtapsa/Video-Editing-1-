The idea

The drain is a line, so the film is built on one. A gold rule sits under every headline. A gold edge sweeps along the drain and turns the tile cover into steel. A gold leader points at the AquaLock trap. A diagonal ivory veil opens the end card. Every move follows the drain's 65° screen angle, which is what makes it feel designed rather than assembled.

Story in four acts. Hook: "Tile it. Or flaunt it." puts the reversible cover in the first 2.5 seconds. Invisible: "Water disappears. So does the drain." turns the original's static opening into the line that carries the film. Engineered: a speed ramp lands the cyan trap ignition on the downbeat and the AquaLock lockup takes the screen. Yours: one frame-matched sweep from tile to steel, the room, then the brand.

Technique sheets

Masked-rise headline (every headline)
  1. PREssential Graphics → Text. Futura PT Demi (Adobe Fonts; Jost SemiBold is the free equivalent) 98 px, hook lines 132 px, All Caps, tracking 15, #231F1F. Top-left at x 118, y 104.
  2. Nest the graphic. Add Transform, then Crop below it. Order matters: Transform moves the text, and Crop stays fixed as the mask.
  3. Crop Bottom until the mask sits just under the descenders.
  4. Transform Position Y: +110 → 0 over 14 f (Bezier, outgoing influence ≈ 90%). Out: 0 → −110 over 8 f, ease-in. Untick "Use Composition's Shutter Angle", Shutter Angle 180.
  5. Gold rule: 180×4 px rectangle #BE8F42, 10 px under the text, Crop Right 100 → 0% from cue +4 f to +22 f.
  6. Label: Futura PT Medium 27 px, tracking 320, #0E5E7E. Opacity 0 → 100 over 21 f from cue +9 f, with Tracking 550 → 320 over the same frames.
Tile → steel transformation (shot 10)
  1. Frame Hold the tile at source 14:21 (V1) and the steel at 20:24 (V2), 56 f each.
  2. Register: V2 Blend Mode → Difference. Nudge V2 Scale +5% relative and Position ≈ −57, −27 px until the floor goes black and only the drain differs. Back to Normal.
  3. V2 → Linear Wipe, Feather 20. Set the angle so the edge sits square to the drain and runs from its lower-left end to its upper-right end (≈ 245°; use 65° if it runs backwards). Completion 100 → 0% from 15:02 to 15:24, ease in/out.
  4. Gold edge: 4×2400 px rectangle #D0A65B, Rotation 65.3°. Glow copy: 26 px wide, #FFF3DC, Gaussian Blur 30, Screen, 85%. Keyframe both so they ride the wipe edge.
  5. Nest V1 + V2 + edges. Scale the nest 155.3 → 162.8 across the shot so the frozen frames still move.
  6. AEOptional extra: CC Light Sweep across the steel as the wipe finishes. The Premiere version holds up without it.
Tracked AquaLock callout (shot 7)

The source camera drifts about 60 px upward during the cutaway, so the pin has to follow the trap.

  • AEDynamic Link the shot, Track Motion on the right wall of the glowing cup, apply to a Null, and parent the pin and the leader's start to it.
  • PRKeyframe the pin's Position: 09:13 → 1179,721 · 10:20 → 1154,664 · 11:24 → 1127,613 · 13:00 → 1112,605, Auto Bezier. Pulse ring: Scale 100 → 440%, Opacity 100 → 0 every 38 f.
  • Leader: a 3 px #D0A65B polyline, revealed with Crop Left 100 → 0 from 09:16 to 09:29.
  • Panel: 540×470 at x 1290, y 250, #1A1717 at 86%, 3 px gold top edge. Slides in from +90 px (09:14–10:00). Inside: AquaLock dark lockup at 440 px, two ✓ bullets 12 f apart, and a gold footer at 11:09.
  • Ignition bloom: duplicate the shot, HSL Secondary key on cyan, Gaussian Blur 80, Screen. Opacity 0 → 100 at 09:13 → 30 by 10:06.
Speed ramps that hit the beat
  • Shot 2, the flip: Time Remapping keyframes at 01:08 (src 16:20), 02:00 (src 17:04) and 02:15 (src 17:17) give 60% through the flip, then 86%. Frame Sampling: Optical Flow.
  • Shot 7, the ignition: keyframes at 08:23 (src 07:24), 09:12 (src 08:22, the first cyan frame) and 13:04 (src 12:17) give 149% then 102%. The trap lights exactly on the downbeat.
  • Shot 11, the room: 74% with Optical Flow. Untick "Maintain Audio Pitch" on slowed clips so the production audio drops in pitch and gains weight.
Colour (Lumetri on one adjustment layer)
  • Basic: Temperature −6 · Contrast +8 · Highlights −6 · Shadows −4 · Whites +6 · Blacks −4 · Saturation 93.
  • RGB Curves: a gentle S (black output to about 1.5%, 22% → 19%, 80% → 83%, white to 99%).
  • Color Wheels: shadows a touch toward blue/cyan (≈ +3), highlights neutral. This turns yellow beige into ivory stone and keeps the steel silver.
  • Shot 7 only: HSL Secondary on cyan, Saturation +12, to match AquaLock #13A3D7.
  • Sharpen 25 (35 for 1:1) to hide the 720p → 1080p upscale. Vignette −0.22, off from 20:08. Optional Noise 2% for texture.
Transitions
  • Hook → act 2 flash (03:20–03:29): #FFF7EA solid, Screen, 0 → 90 → 0%, hard cut underneath at 03:23.
  • Whip (13:04–13:10): Directional Blur Length 0 → 40 → 0 plus a 2-frame warm flash at 35%.
  • Ivory veil (19:26–20:08): #F4EFE8 matte with Linear Wipe at the shot 10 angle, Feather 30. It then dissolves (20:09–21:00) into a gradient veil, solid over the top-left 40% and fading out parallel to the drain.
